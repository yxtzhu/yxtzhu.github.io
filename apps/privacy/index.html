Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2026

This privacy policy applies to the personal tools and applications hosted on or associated with tingtingzhu.com, including a Pinterest board viewer application used solely by the site owner.

What this app does

This application accesses Pinterest board data exclusively through the Pinterest API, using the site owner's own authenticated credentials. It is a personal tool and is not available to the public as a service.

Data collection

This application does not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors or users. No accounts are created, no cookies are set for tracking purposes, and no analytics are gathered.

Pinterest data

The application reads pin image URLs and descriptions from the site owner's own Pinterest boards. This data is not shared with any third parties and is used solely for personal display purposes.

Third-party services
